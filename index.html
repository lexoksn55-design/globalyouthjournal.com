Empowering Young Minds, One Paper at a Time
GlobalYouthJournal is a youth-led initiative providing a FREE platform for student researchers and journalists to publish their work. We support emerging voices through a structured student review process that emphasizes academic integrity, clarity, and constructive feedback. By combining accessibility with editorial standards, we aim to cultivate high-quality student publications across disciplines.

About Us

The GlobalYouthJournal is a youth-led initiative that allows young, skilled researchers and journalists to publish their work without any fees, only a short review period. This website exists for youth, made by youth. We aim to help you achieve your goals with minimal restrictions.
